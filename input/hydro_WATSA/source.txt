====================================================================
hydro_WATSA -- Swiss hydro fleet data pipeline for FEM
====================================================================
Last updated: 2026-08-03.
Purpose: build, from public sources, (a) a validated per-plant table
of the Swiss hydro fleet incl. storage energy content, (b) clustered
representative plants for the FEM electricity market model, and
(c) weekly natural-inflow profiles per cluster for the model's
weather years 1995 / 2008 / 2009.

Reading order for a new person:
  1. this file, top to bottom
  2. validation_report_hydro_storage.md  (what was wrong and fixed)
  3. the docstring of each script, in pipeline order (Section 2)
  4. cluster_sweep/sweep_convergence.png and
     inflow_profiles/inflow_profiles_n12.png  (what the results look like)

2. PIPELINE, STEP BY STEP
--------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1  extract_wasta.py
  Source: BFE WASTA "Statistik der Wasserkraftanlagen der Schweiz",
  Stand 1.1.2026 (plants >= 300 kW). Downloaded 2026-07-16 from
  https://pubdb.bfe.admin.ch/de/publication/download/12596
  (landing: https://opendata.swiss/de/dataset/statistik-der-wasserkraftanlagen-wasta)
  Keeps ~30 columns (capacities, expected annual/winter/summer
  generation, pumping, heads, coordinates LV95, type), maps WASTA
  types to model technologies: L->ror, S->dam, P/PS->psp_open,
  U->psp_close. No filtering at this stage.
  Sanity anchors (CH rows): ~17.2 GW turbines, ~4.0 GW pumps,
  ~37 TWh/a expected generation.

STEP 2  scrape_wiki_speicherseen.py
  Scrapes lake volumes [Mio m3] and full-supply levels [m a.s.l.]
  from https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liste_der_Speicherseen_in_der_Schweiz
  (community-maintained; large lakes spot-checked). WASTA does NOT
  contain reservoir volumes/energies -- that gap is why steps 2-3 exist.
  manual_lakes.csv supplements lakes absent from that list: the KHR
  reservoirs Lago di Lei (197 Mio m3 @ 1931 m; Italian territory, but
  Swiss-operated and counted in the BFE statistics, exactly like Lago
  di Livigno), Sufnersee (16 @ 1401) and Stausee Baerenburg (1 @ 1080).
  Values from the operator: https://www.khr.ch/de/stauseen

STEP 3  estimate_storage_energy.py
  Physics estimate per lake:  E = V * 0.0023 kWh/m3/m * head,
  head = lake level minus turbine axis of the LOWEST stage of the
  cascade chain fed by the lake (full-cascade convention -- see
  limitations). Chains are found by name-matching lakes against the
  WASTA water bodies, then expanding over shared scheme (WKA) name
  tokens. lake_plant_overrides.csv pins problem cases; overrides are
  applied BEFORE the cascade expansion so the override plant's whole
  scheme is found.
  Calibration: raw estimates are scaled PER REGION (Wallis,
  Graubuenden, Tessin, UebrigCH) to the BFE maximum usable energy
  content ("Fuellungsgrad der Speicherseen", ogd17,
  https://www.uvek-gis.admin.ch/BFE/ogd/17/ogd17_fuellungsgrad_speicherseen.csv),
  so regional totals match the official 8,895 GWh by construction and
  the physics only decides the split within a region. Factors after
  validation: Wallis 0.88, Graubuenden 0.86, Tessin 1.04, UebrigCH
  0.78 -- all <= ~1 except Tessin, consistent with usable < gross
  volume and the assumed eta = 0.85.
  Anchors: Lac des Dix 1,529 vs 1,500 GWh (SWV factsheet
  https://www.hydrosuisse.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/site/PDF/FB_SWV-Speichersee_Energiewende-2022.pdf);
  independent cross-check candidates: JRC hydro-power database
  (https://github.com/energy-modelling-toolkit/hydro-power-database),
  GloHydroRes (Shah et al. 2025).

STEP 4  build_individual_plant_table.py
  One row per Swiss plant in operation. Each lake's calibrated energy
  is split over the storage-type turbine stages of its chain below
  the lake level, proportional to stage head; ror stages get 0
  (fixed infeed in the model). Regional rescale so sums match ogd17
  exactly.
  The table carries TWO classifications: "type" is the WASTA physical
  class (never overwritten), "category" is the MODEL treatment:
    - storage-capable plants with turbines but E/P < 24 h (zero OR
      near-zero storage: Hopflauenen Trift, Innertkirchen 2, Isenthal
      Bolzbach, Engeweiher, Trubsee, Vouvry, and other small ones;
      175 MW turbines, 8 MW pumps) -> "ror" = fixed infeed
    - pump-ONLY stations (WASTA type P, no turbine: 20 intra-cascade
      transfer pumps like Cleuson -> Dix, 227 MW) -> "pump_station",
      EXCLUDED from the clusters: their lifted water is already
      contained in the downstream plants' production expectations,
      and modelling them as market pumping would misrepresent
      scheduled transfers as price arbitrage. Their 227 MW are
      RE-ATTRIBUTED to the psp_open clusters' pump capacity
      (storage-weighted) in the representative tables: FEM's current
      PECD source counts them inside the pumped_open aggregate
      (10,732 MW incl. transfer pumps), so dropping them entirely
      would silently shrink national pump capacity vs today's model.
      They still do NOT enter the clustering itself (no turbines,
      no features).
  All downstream scripts read "category", never re-derive it.
  All CSVs are written as UTF-8 with BOM (utf-8-sig) so Excel on
  Windows displays umlauts correctly.

STEP 5  cluster_hydro_plants.py  (design decided 2026-07-30/31)
  Features -- ONLY quantities that map to model parameters or the
  cluster's inflow profile:
    log10 E/P [h]      storage speed -> water-value dynamics (log
                       because k-means needs it for a 3-orders-of-
                       magnitude skewed feature and storage classes
                       are multiplicative: daily/weekly/monthly/seasonal)
    regime proxy       summer-half-year inflow share, piecewise-linear
                       in intake altitude (axis + gross head),
                       CALIBRATED AT RUNTIME on the WASTA ror plants
                       (ror has no storage, so its winter/summer
                       generation split observes inflow seasonality
                       directly): 390m->0.54 ... 2369m->0.88
    pump ratio         pump / (pump + turbine)
  Capacity is the k-means sample weight, NOT a feature. Size, head,
  altitude were removed as features (they already entered via the
  storage estimate; clusters that differ only in size/geography have
  identical water-value dynamics).
  Budget rule: every category (dam/ror/psp_open/psp_close) gets 1
  cluster as representation floor; the remaining n-4 go
  proportionally to STORAGE ENERGY CONTENT [GWh], largest remainder.
  Rationale: clusters buy water-value resolution, water values attach
  to stored energy; capacity- or generation-proportional rules
  provably misallocate (generation gives half the budget to ror,
  whose fixed infeed enters the model only as a SUM, so extra ror
  clusters cannot change results).
  Reclassification: S-plants with E/P < 24 h (Hopflauenen Trift,
  Innertkirchen 2 -- daily basins, no seasonal reservoir) are
  clustered with ror. WASTA type stays in "type", clustering category
  in "category".
  Naming: dam_1..k ascending in aggregate E/P (dam_1 = fastest).
  n = 4 gives a single dam class = the EuroMod-failure exhibit.
  n-selection: sweep_convergence.png shows the dam E/P ladder per n;
  distinct up to n ~ 10-11, near-duplicates from n ~ 12-13. Final
  choice by water-value distinctness in FEM runs.

STEP 6  build_inflow_profiles.py  (three layers)
  SHAPE  from 101 near-natural CAMELS-CH donor catchments
         (observation-based daily discharge, calendar years 1995/
         2008/2009; selection: CH, no hydropower, no reservoirs, no
         lake outlets, readme exclusions, <= 5% missing per year,
         strictly positive annual volume per year -- gauge 2079
         Le Prese reports 0.0 for all of 1995, bad data coded as
         zeros, now rejected by the gate).
         Each dam/psp_open plant matched to its SINGLE most similar
         donor by |intake altitude - catchment mean elevation|
         (scale 300 m) and distance (scale 50 km); one donor keeps
         the full weather variability of a real catchment instead of
         averaging it away (user decision 2026-08-03; --n-donors
         restores blending if ever wanted). Worst elevation mismatch
         237 m, p90 187 m.
         CAMELS-CH: Hoege et al. 2023, https://zenodo.org/records/15025258,
         CC-BY 4.0 (attribution required in the paper).
  SPLIT  cluster aggregation weighted by NATURAL annual inflow =
         WASTA expected generation - 0.757 x pump demand (FEM's
         0.87 turbine x 0.87 pump; pumped-through energy is not
         natural inflow; psp_open natural share drops 5.6% -> 4.5%
         of the national total).
  LEVEL  reconciled week-by-week to the PECD CH00 inflow series of
         the same weather year (PECD 2021.3 parquet mirror,
         https://zenodo.org/record/7224854; note ALL Swiss natural
         inflow sits under technology 'pumped_open' there, the
         'reservoir' rows are zero). Annual totals: 15.9 / 18.6 /
         18.3 TWh for 1995 / 2008 / 2009 (1995 is a ~13% dry year).
         This keeps the national series identical to what the TYNDP-
         based neighbour data assumes.
  Week convention: week = (day_of_year - 1)//7 + 1, 53 weeks,
  matching the PECD series. FEM's timemap (timemaps_hydro_year.csv,
  model year starts t_6553 = Oct 1) wraps calendar years into the
  Oct-Sep hydrological year -- profiles therefore stay calendar-year.
  Diagnostics: reconciliation factor f(w) = PECD share / gauge share,
  median 0.73-0.87 across the three years, p10-p90 ~0.25-2.0.
  NOT a week-alignment artifact (lag
  scan flat): PECD is more summer-peaked (winter share 0.14, peak
  wk 28) than any near-natural gauge blend (0.29, wk 22) because
  storage intake catchments sit higher and are more glaciated than
  the near-natural gauge network reaches. Design: PECD is trusted for
  the national shape, gauges only decide the split BETWEEN clusters.
  Between-cluster differentiation survives: winter inflow shares at
  n=12 run 0.07 (glacial) .. 0.30 (low-altitude) around PECD's 0.14.

4. KNOWN LIMITATIONS (state these in the paper)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Full-cascade head convention: each lake is valued down to the
  lowest stage of its chain. Under per-lake accounting (SWV style)
  Lago di Lei is ~200 GWh, here 494; Muttsee analogous. Neutralized
  in totals by the regional calibration, but the within-region split
  leans on this convention.
- ~107 GWh of unmatched run-of-river weirs (Wohlensee, Niederried,
  Klingnau, ...) is smeared over matched plants by the calibration
  (~1% of total). Consider dropping them from the lake list.
- Within-cluster over-flexibility remains: summed capacity ignores
  individual reservoir bounds and cascade coupling (aggregation
  literature; bilevel-fitted equivalents are the refinement path).
- Uniform weekly reconciliation factor: the PECD-vs-gauge shape
  correction is applied identically to all clusters; cluster-specific
  glacier corrections would need a hydrological model.
- Observed 1995 hydrographs embed the 1995 glacier state; the
  planned Hydro-CH2018 delta-change sensitivity addresses the trend.
- Pump round-trip 0.757 (= FEM's 0.87 turbine x 0.87 pump) in the
  natural-inflow correction.
- The planned Trift reservoir (~85 Mio m3, KWO, delayed/contested)
  is NOT included -- a 2035/2050 scenario decision, not a data gap.
- Regime-proxy calibration: low-altitude nodes are slightly flattened
  by alpine-fed river ror; top bin is thin (7 plants). The proxy only
  orders plants for clustering; profiles come from gauges.

5. FEM WIRING (implemented 2026-08-03)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The model consumes this folder through ONE setting: "hydro_clusters"
(row in scenarios/scen_to_run_*.csv; default "legacy" in
scenarios/settings_default.py).
  legacy    the old PECD representation: CH00_dam (0 MW),
            CH00_psp_close, large_psp/medium_reservior/small_reservior
            with PECD inflow shares. Bit-identical to the pre-wiring
            code (regression-tested).
  4..20     WASTA cluster representation: replaces the WHOLE Swiss
            storage fleet with the n-cluster plants
            (CH00_dam_1, ..., CH00_psp_open_1, ..., CH00_psp_close_1,
            ...; the CH00_ prefix makes node attribution and
            price-setting classification work unchanged). Capacities/
            storage from cluster_sweep/representative_hydro_plants_
            n{N}.csv; weekly inflow per cluster from inflow_profiles/
            cluster_inflow_weekly.csv (same 53-week convention as
            PECD, national total identical to legacy by construction).
            Swiss ror is untouched (PECD infeed path + ror_annual_TWh
            knob; the ror cluster of the table is deliberately not
            imported -- it would double count).
Touched files: model/data_import_fcns.py (_hydro_clusters_active,
read_hydro_cluster_table, read_hydro_capacities,
read_inflow_data_hourly), data_prep/data_import_TYNDP.py (pass-
through), scenarios/settings_default.py, scenarios/
scen_to_run_STORSUPPORT.csv (new row, set to legacy).
BFE knobs with clusters: hydro_inflow_TWh scales all cluster inflows
to the target (identical week-53 convention as legacy);
pump_capacity_GW scales all CH00_psp_* cluster pumps proportionally;
adding_hydro_storage_cap_TWh distributes over all cluster storage
proportionally.
Tested (sandbox, no solver): legacy = old code bit-for-bit; n=4..20 x
weather years 1995/2008/2009 all load; totals match the tables and
the legacy national inflow; knobs verified (4.71 GW, +1 TWh, 19 TWh).
Reporting generalised 2026-08-04: detailed_reporting/ (constants.py,
Output_Spatial.py, Output_Temp.py) now sums legacy + cluster plant
names (absent names contribute 0, so the union works for both
representations), and aggregation/aggregate_new.py mappings_merge was
rebuilt as a per-index set union -- the old positional combine_first
silently dropped plants that only exist in later scenarios, which
emptied the CH hydro rows of Annual_balance_ch.csv in cluster runs.
Additionally price_setting_tech.py now writes
price_setting_plants_count_comarginal.csv (per label: Hours_present,
Share_frac_role = tied hours split equally among the role classes
present, Share_frac_unit = candidate-count weighted) as the
robustness companion to the winner-takes-all count -- the CH-vs-
abroad tie-break is positional, so winner counts alone overstate
whoever wins ties.
